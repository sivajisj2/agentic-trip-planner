# Group votes and conflict resolution

Record votes with a participant identifier and place identifier. Each participant may cast at most one positive vote per place unless the group explicitly adopts another voting system. Do not invent ballots. Separate `not yet voted` from a real `no` vote. Explain how duplicate votes are resolved. Use vote counts in the ranking only after confirming the source of the votes.

Flag conflicts when one member's hard constraints are violated, individual budget caps differ, or category preference ratings differ by >=3. Show at least two alternatives with tradeoffs. If no consensus is reached, offer a short ballot and wait for user-provided results. Do not claim a final democratic decision without evidence of participant responses.
