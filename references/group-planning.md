# Group requirements and preference collection

Collect: destination/region, local dates, travelers, origin, budget/currency, accommodation and transport needs, mobility/accessibility constraints, interests, hard exclusions, and decision maker. Do not ask for sensitive personal information unless strictly necessary.

Represent travelers as pseudonymous IDs. For each person record categories and preference weights 0–5, must-do activities, hard constraints, and budget cap. Aggregate category preferences as an explicitly disclosed mean or median; default to mean of supplied ratings, not imagined votes. If people disagree strongly (e.g. difference >=3 on a 0–5 scale), flag a conflict and propose alternatives. A hard constraint is never overridden by a high score.

State whether inputs are supplied, assumed, or unknown. Ask targeted follow-up questions when missing data would materially change a decision. If budget is shared, clarify whether it includes transport to the destination.
